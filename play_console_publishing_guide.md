## 2. Create a Google Play Developer Account

> [!NOTE]
> Skip this section if you already have a Google Play Developer account.

1. Go to **[Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/signup)**
2. Sign in with your Google Account (`decodersifat@gmail.com`)
3. Accept the **Developer Distribution Agreement**
4. Pay the **$25 one-time registration fee**
5. Fill in your developer profile:
   - **Developer Name**: `Ovvash Bondhu`
   - **Email address**: `decodersifat@gmail.com`
   - **Phone number**: Your phone number
   - **Website** (optional): `https://ovvash-bondhu.blogspot.com`
6. **Verify your identity** (ID verification is now mandatory for new accounts)
7. Wait for account approval (usually 24-48 hours for new accounts)

## 11. Generate Signed Release APK/AAB

### Step 1: Create a Release Keystore (One-Time)

> [!CAUTION]
> **BACK UP YOUR KEYSTORE FILE AND PASSWORDS!** If you lose them, you can never update your app.

Open **Terminal / PowerShell** and run:

```bash
keytool -genkey -v -keystore ovvash-bondhu-release.keystore -alias ovvash-bondhu -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 25000 -storepass YOUR_STORE_PASSWORD -keypass YOUR_KEY_PASSWORD
```

Fill in the prompts:
```
What is your first and last name? → Your Name
What is the name of your organizational unit? → Development
What is the name of your organization? → Ovvash Bondhu
What is the name of your City or Locality? → Your City
What is the name of your State or Province? → Your State
What is the two-letter country code? → BD
```

### Step 2: Configure Signing in Android Studio

1. Open Android Studio
2. Go to **Build → Generate Signed Bundle / APK**
3. Select **"Android App Bundle"** (AAB is required by Google Play)
4. Click **Next**
5. Fill in:
   - **Key store path**: Path to your `.keystore` file
   - **Key store password**: Your store password
   - **Key alias**: `ovvash-bondhu`
   - **Key password**: Your key password
6. Click **Next**
7. Select **"release"** build variant
8. Click **"Create"**

### Step 3: Locate the AAB File

The signed AAB will be at:
```
e:\AndroidStudio\OvvashBondhu\OvvashBondhu\app\release\app-release.aab
```

## 12. Upload to Play Console

### Step 1: Set Up App Signing (Play App Signing)

Navigate to: **Release → Setup → App signing**

- **Enroll in Play App Signing** (strongly recommended — Google manages your signing key)
- Upload your upload key or let Google generate one

### Step 2: Create an Internal Testing Release (Recommended First)

Navigate to: **Release → Testing → Internal testing**

1. Click **"Create new release"**
2. Upload your `app-release.aab` file
3. **Release name**: `1.0.0 (1)` (auto-filled from your versionName/versionCode)
4. **Release notes**:

```
Ovvash Bondhu v1.0.0 — Initial Release 🎉

• Create and track daily habits with flexible goal types
• Build streaks with daily check-ins and mood tracking
• View progress with calendar and analytics
• Connect with friends for accountability
• Create and join groups (up to 50 members)
• Take on time-bound challenges together
• Send encouragements and reactions
• Beautiful Material 3 design with Light/Dark themes
• Full English and বাংলা (Bangla) language support
• Complete privacy controls and account deletion
```

5. Click **"Review release"** → **"Start rollout to Internal testing"**

### Step 3: Add Testers

1. Go to **Internal testing → Testers** tab
2. Create an email list (e.g., "Internal Testers")
3. Add tester emails
4. Share the **opt-in link** with your testers

### Step 4: Promote to Production

Once testing is satisfactory:

Navigate to: **Release → Production**

1. Click **"Create new release"**
2. Upload the same AAB (or promote from internal testing)
3. Add release notes (same as above)
4. Click **"Review release"**
5. Click **"Start rollout to Production"**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Choose rollout percentage:
> - **Staged rollout**: Start with 20% → 50% → 100% (recommended for first release)
> - **Full rollout**: 100% immediately

### Firebase Console Setup

> [!WARNING]
> You must add your **release keystore SHA-1 fingerprint** to Firebase Console for Google Sign-In to work in the production build.

1. Get your release keystore SHA-1:
```bash
keytool -list -v -keystore ovvash-bondhu-release.keystore -alias ovvash-bondhu
```
2. Go to **[Firebase Console](https://console.firebase.google.com)** → Project `ovvash-bondhu-feae6`
3. Go to **Project Settings → General → Your apps → Android app**
4. Click **"Add fingerprint"**
5. Paste your **release SHA-1** fingerprint
6. Also add the **SHA-1 from Play App Signing** (found in Play Console → Setup → App signing → App signing key certificate → SHA-1)
7. Download the updated `google-services.json` and replace it in your project

## 15. Post-Launch Maintenance

### After Your App Goes Live:

1. **Monitor Crash Reports**: Firebase Crashlytics + Play Console → Android Vitals
2. **Reply to User Reviews**: Play Console → Ratings and reviews
3. **Track Install Metrics**: Play Console → Statistics
4. **Update Regularly**: Fix bugs, add features, keep `targetSdk` current
5. **Version Updates**:
   - Increment `versionCode` by 1 for each update
   - Update `versionName` (e.g., `"1.0"` → `"1.1"`)
   - Upload new AAB
   - Add release notes describing changes

### SEO & ASO (App Store Optimization)

- Include relevant keywords in your short/long description
- Regularly update screenshots for new features
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)
- Keep a consistent update schedule
